1. Strategic positioning

Verbal roadmap (Jobs structure: Problem → Solution → Meaning)

Problem

80% of AI projects die before production. Not because models fail — because the data underneath isn't ready.

Solution

A semantic layer that resolves what "customer," "claim," "revenue" mean across every entity in the portfolio. Once. Reused.

Meaning

AI moves from pilot purgatory to production. Each bolt-on becomes AI-ready in weeks. The investment compounds across the portfolio.

Headline rationale

"Models work. Data doesn't." mirrors the rhetorical pattern already used on healthcare-state-rhtp ("Transport is solved. Semantics is not."). Three beats. Concedes what the buyer believes (AI is real, models work) and redirects to the actual obstacle.

Brand tagline

The semantic data layer that makes AI work across your portfolio.

Section 2 — The AI ROI Gap (inline stats, pain, badge: "The Problem")

Heading: The AI ROI Gap

  • 80% — projects that never reach production
  • $187B — invested in AI in 2024 with no clear returns
  • $2.3M — average cost of one failed AI POC

Note: The investor-vs-CEO line was originally drafted as the annotation under these stats. It has been moved to the DarkReveal beat further down the page (Section 7) to serve as the dramatic emotional pivot rather than a quiet footnote.

Section 3 — Why It's Not the Model (N × M chaos visualization)

Heading: Why It's Not the Model

Lede above the visual:

Every acquisition adds its own ERP, CRM, EHR, billing platform — each with its own definition of "customer," "claim," "revenue." N entities × M systems creates exponential integration debt. AI built on that debt can't ship.

Three supporting bullets:

  • 70–85% of AI failures trace to data, not models — Gartner, McKinsey, BCG, and RAND converge here independently.
  • The integration matrix grows multiplicatively, not additively — each bolt-on touches every system already in the platform.
  • Pilot purgatory traps two-thirds of portcos — POCs work on one CRM or EHR. Production exposes the fragmentation that was invisible in the pilot.

Section 4 — The Semantic Layer (hub-and-spoke vs. tangle)

Heading: One layer. Every entity.

Body:

Instead of building point-to-point integrations between every new acquisition and every existing system, the semantic layer becomes the shared meaning across them. "Customer" means the same thing once — and AI runs against that.

Section 5 — What Changes (AccelerationRows, solution)

Heading: What changes when the data layer comes first.

Three acceleration rows:

  • Time-to-AI-ready: Months → Weeks per portco onboarded
  • Pilot survival: Pilot → Production for POCs that previously stalled
  • Per-acquisition cost curve: Compounding → Flat as the portfolio scales

Section 6 — Why the Math Works (AiCompoundingCurve, proof)

Heading: Why the math changes.

Lede:

Without a shared layer, every new portco re-pays the integration cost. With one, portco #10 doesn't re-map portcos 1–9. The cost curve flattens instead of compounding.

Three proof points (rendered as labels on the curve):

  • One semantic layer — shared across every entity
  • Bi-directional — AI outputs flow back into the operational systems your portcos already run
  • Resilient — new acquisitions absorb without re-architecting

Section 7 — The ROI-gap punchline (DarkReveal)

Three sequential reveals on a dark background, paced by scroll:

  • Setup: Investors expect AI ROI in 6 months.
  • Reveal: CEOs need 2–4 years.
  • Punchline (red): Only one variable closes that gap. The data layer.

5.4 The call-CTA component change

Extend MicrositeEmailGate with a mode prop (default 'paper'):

interface MicrositeEmailGateProps {
  slug: string;
  formspreeId: string;
  ctaLabel?: string;
  // New:
  mode?: 'paper' | 'call';
  papers?: MicrositePaper[];      // required when mode === 'paper'
  callConfirmation?: string;      // shown when mode === 'call' and unlocked
}

In mode: 'call' the post-submit "unlocked" state renders the confirmation copy instead of paper download links. Defaulting mode to 'paper' means every existing microsite keeps working unchanged.
