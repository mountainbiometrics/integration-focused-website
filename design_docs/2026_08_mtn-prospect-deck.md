## 17 — Mountain Pathways (Summit · 1 of 3)

**The path through your operation, made navigable.**

Pathways turns fragmented operational data into a model of how work actually moves: the entities, the stages they pass through, and the events that move them. People and agents can see where something is now, what happened before it, and what usually happens next.

**WHAT IT ANSWERS**
- What is the process, and what are its stages?
- Which events define each stage, and where does the handoff happen?
- Which past cases look like this one?
- Where does acting actually change the outcome?

---

## 18 — Frontier Models (Summit · 2 of 3)

**Order matters. These models learn from it.**

Sequence models trained on the customer's own timelines. They learn which events matter to each other across time, instead of scoring a single moment in isolation.

**WHAT IT DOES**
- Learns from sequences of events, not from snapshots
- Combines different kinds of data once each is represented as tokens
- Anticipates the likely next step along a timeline
- Becomes viable only once the pathway data is clean and consistent

---

## 19 — Mountain Environments (Summit · 3 of 3)

**Agents practice before they perform.**

A simulation and evaluation environment built from the customer's own pathway, data structure, and operating rules. Agents are trained, scored, and governed there before they touch production.

**WHAT IT PROVES**
- Can the agent complete the task at all?
- Does it use the right evidence and follow policy?
- Does it know when to escalate, and to whom?
- Can we show performance before anything reaches production?
